ICEAS 2022 17-18 November 2022, Tirana

The Polytechnic University of Tirana and the Faculty of Mathematics Engineering and Physics Engineering are pleased to announce the 3rd International Conference on Applied Sciences and Engineering 2022 (ICEAS 2022). The conference proposes multidisciplinary forum and it is aimed at presenting researches in progress, theoretical overviews or comparative studies in the fields of applied maths, physics and chemistry towards application in industry.
